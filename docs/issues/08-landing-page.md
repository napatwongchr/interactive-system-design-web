# Issue 08 — Landing Page

**Type: HITL** — design review required before merge.

## What to build

Build the landing page that introduces the platform to vibe coders who are discovering it for the first time. The page should communicate who it's for, what they'll learn, and why it's different from every other system design resource. The visual style sets the tone for the whole product — bold, illustrated, playful, not a dev tutorial.

Key sections: hero (what is this + CTA to start learning), "who is this for" (validates the vibe coder), curriculum arc preview (shows the 6-chapter scale journey), and a footer.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Hero section includes headline, subheadline, and a CTA button that navigates to the first lesson
- [ ] "Who is this for" section speaks directly to vibe coders (references their experience without CS jargon)
- [ ] Curriculum arc section previews all 6 chapters with their scale milestones
- [ ] Visual style matches the bold/illustrated/playful direction (consistent with lesson page styling)
- [ ] Page is responsive on desktop (primary target) and does not break on mobile
- [ ] No login or sign-up required — CTA goes straight to lesson 1
- [ ] Design has been reviewed and approved by a human before merge (HITL)
